# 02 — District Specifications

## Spring — Overgrown Suburb and School

### Role

First major district. Teaches exploration, environmental reading, basic shortcut unlocking, and manageable outdoor combat.

### Visual identity

- Dense vegetation reclaiming suburban roads.
- Wet asphalt, moss, flowering trees, vines, and damaged gardens.
- Soft green daylight with humid fog.
- Clear silhouettes for school, water tower, and greenhouse roofs.

### Major POIs

1. Overgrown residential block.
2. Public park.
3. School and gymnasium.
4. Greenhouses.
5. Maintenance tunnel entrance.

### Main objective

Restore the school communication relay and retrieve a district access key.

### Combat structure

- Residential ambush route with low zombie density.
- School interior with short sightlines.
- Gymnasium horde arena with two exits and an unlockable upper gallery route.
- Park used as a lower-pressure traversal and scavenging loop.

### Required shortcuts

- School service gate to the Hub road.
- Greenhouse drainage tunnel to the sewer network.
- Collapsed garden wall connecting suburb and park.

## Summer — Beachfront and Boardwalk

### Role

Open-space pressure, ranged sightlines, vehicle obstacles, rooftop traversal, and resource collection.

### Visual identity

- Harsh sunlight filtered by smoke.
- Salt-damaged buildings, faded signage, sand drifts, wrecked cars, and amusement structures.
- Orange, cyan, and sun-bleached concrete palette.

### Major POIs

1. Gas station.
2. Motel.
3. Large parking lot.
4. Boardwalk.
5. Beach service buildings.
6. Small amusement pier.

### Main objective

Recover fuel and a compatible battery, then activate the service-road gate.

### Combat structure

- Parking lot as a large flanking arena.
- Motel rooms for close-quarters scavenging.
- Boardwalk pressure event with lateral movement and limited cover.
- Rooftop route between motel and shops.

### Required shortcuts

- Motel fire escape.
- Storm drain to sewer junction.
- Boardwalk maintenance gate to the Hub ring road.

## Autumn — Town Center and Market

### Role

Dense urban navigation, interiors, alleys, vertical threats, and key-item progression.

### Visual identity

- Orange foliage, wet brick, smoke, abandoned market stalls, and old civic architecture.
- Strong contrast between warm exterior light and dark interiors.

### Major POIs

1. Church square.
2. Market hall.
3. Apartment block.
4. Town hall.
5. Police station.
6. Clinic connecting toward the central district.

### Main objective

Recover police armory access and municipal infrastructure records.

### Combat structure

- Alley ambushes and sound-driven interior threats.
- Church square circular horde arena.
- Apartment vertical route with rooftop escape.
- Police station defense event with controlled reinforcement waves.

### Required shortcuts

- Market loading corridor.
- Apartment rooftop bridge.
- Police garage gate to the central road.

## Winter — Industrial Zone

### Role

Highest sustained difficulty before extraction. Industrial hazards, machinery, power restoration, and large zombie pressure.

### Visual identity

- Snow, ice, steam, blue industrial lighting, frozen water, and heavy steel structures.
- Warm emergency lights indicate interactable objectives.

### Major POIs

1. Warehouse.
2. Loading yard.
3. Maintenance workshops.
4. Power station.
5. Cooling channel.
6. Sewer control room.

### Main objective

Restart the power station, restore the dam controls, and unlock the extraction route.

### Combat structure

- Warehouse maze with multiple aisles and elevated catwalks.
- Loading yard as the largest standard horde arena.
- Power station multi-stage defense with moving objective positions.
- Frozen maintenance route that becomes unsafe during the final phase.

### Required shortcuts

- Warehouse conveyor bridge.
- Utility tunnel to the sewer control room.
- Service road directly toward the dam.

## Hub Safehouse

### Functions

- Initial spawn and tutorial.
- Equipment preparation.
- Objective display.
- District status visualization.
- Optional storage and upgrade stations if supported by existing mechanics.
- Four outward routes with readable seasonal landmarks.

The Hub must be compact enough to cross in under 45 seconds and protected from routine zombie navigation.

## River Dam Extraction

### Sequence

1. Reach the dam control perimeter.
2. Restore or confirm power.
3. Open the upper access route.
4. Defend two changing positions.
5. Cross the dam while the rear route collapses or is overrun.
6. Reach the helipad or escape vehicle.

The finale must last approximately 8–12 minutes and reuse previously learned traversal, defense, and route-reading skills.
